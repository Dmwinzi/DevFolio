Projects

Most recent work

Mombo SACCO — Microservices Re-Architecture

Led the re-architecture of a legacy monolithic Spring Boot application into containerized, independently deployable microservices. Deployed to AWS (EC2, ECS, RDS) with Kubernetes orchestration and Jenkins CI/CD, reducing deployment time from hours to minutes.



Craft Silicon — Mobile Banking Platform

Built and maintained mobile banking applications used by hundreds of thousands of bank customers, including NCBA. Implemented core banking payment channels (RTGS, EFT, inter-bank transfers) and white-label theming across Android native and Flutter.



M-Zawadi — AI-Powered Vehicle Diagnostics

Designed and built a Flutter application with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) integration for real-time OBD-II vehicle diagnostics, including an AI-powered layer translating raw sensor data into plain-language insights, and a live telemetry dashboard.



EABIRI — Matatu Seat Reservation & Payments

End-to-end Flutter and Spring Boot platform for long-distance matatu bookings, integrating the M-Pesa Daraja API for real-time seat reservation, payments, and digital receipts.



SCAPAY — Scan-to-Pay Platform

Customer-to-business scan-to-pay platform that modernises merchant payments through QR code scanning, focused on security, transaction speed, and a frictionless checkout experience.



CurioCity — Geo-Marketing Platform

Sole Flutter developer on an Australian geo-marketing platform, delivering the app end-to-end with BLoC architecture, real-time GPS geo-fencing, an interactive Google Maps discovery interface, and an analytics dashboard for campaign engagement, deployed on AWS EC2/S3 with a GitHub Actions CI/CD pipeline.



ROOMAR — AR Education Platform

Augmented reality education application enabling teachers to host interactive sessions where they share 3D models with students in real time, similar to a video call but enriched with AR objects for immersive, hands-on learning.



ALGOBOT — Enterprise Voice Assistant

Voice-activated conversational AI assistant designed for enterprise integration, enabling staff to interact with internal systems through natural language commands, improving accessibility and operational efficiency within organisations.



Duke Movers — Logistics Platform

Map-integrated Android logistics application connecting customers with vetted drivers for moving and transportation services. Features real-time driver tracking via Google Maps SDK, route optimisation, job assignment, and a Firebase-powered backend for live updates.



Affecto Foundation — Sponsor Platform

Philanthropic mobile platform enabling sponsors to monitor sponsored children's academic progress and make direct M-Pesa donations. Includes an admin portal for the foundation to manage sponsor relationships and upload school reports.
